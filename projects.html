WiFi Finder

01

A community-powered WiFi map of cafés, restaurants, bars, hotels, libraries, airports and thousands of other places. Every nearby hotspot shows up on a live map, and every password in the app was added by someone who actually connected.

No account, no sign-up, no email. Just open the app and start finding free internet.

Visit wififinder.ca ↗

Hotspots

Languages

Puzzle Rush

02

A fast-paced block-swapping puzzle game in the spirit of Tetris Attack and Pokémon Puzzle League. Swap blocks to make matches, chain combos together, and send garbage blocks across to your opponent before your own stack hits the top.

Battle a cast of animal characters in 3D worlds from a sunlit forest to a misty beaver pond, solve puzzles on planets across the solar system, or race the clock for a spot on the global Time Trial leaderboards.

Visit puzzlerush.app ↗
Modes
Versus AI (easy, medium, hard), Puzzle, Endless, Time Trial
Community
Level editor, shared puzzles, Puzzle of the Day, leaderboards
